(e.g. "X Bot" or "Y Bot"). Save into `assets/anims/mixamo_raw/`, any file name; `organize_mixamo.py` cleans up.

Interact: Picking Up Object, Reach Out, Opening Door, Pushing, Pulling, Drinking, Looking Through Binoculars, Sitting Down, Stand Up
Fight: Sword And Shield Slash (or Standing Melee Attack Horizontal), Punch, Kick, Block, Hit Reaction (Receive Hit), Stabbing, Cast Spell
Reactions: Fear / Terrified, Surprised, Crying, Victory, Cheering, Defeat
